# 🚧 Blocked Drain Civic Action Platform

> **SC-08 — Citizen Reporting & Accountability for Canal and Storm-Drain Blockages**

An intelligent, location-aware civic reporting platform that transforms a citizen's blocked-drain report into a structured and trackable civic ticket.

The platform captures a **photo, description, and GPS location**, automatically identifies the responsible ward using **PostGIS**, checks for nearby unresolved reports, and makes reports available through a **public map and authority dashboard**.

## 🎯 Problem

Blocked canals and storm drains can cause:

- Waterlogging
- Local flooding
- Waste accumulation
- Stagnant water
- Unpleasant odour
- Public health concerns

Citizens may also have difficulty identifying the responsible ward or authority and often have limited visibility after reporting an issue.

### The problem

```text
Citizen sees blockage
        ↓
Doesn't know responsible authority
        ↓
Calls / visits an office
        ↓
No structured ticket
        ↓
Limited status visibility
        ↓
Problem may remain unresolved
